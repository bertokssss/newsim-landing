Philippine Data Privacy Act (RA 10173)

NEWSIM Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how the NEWSIM Maritime Training Management System collects, processes, stores, and protects personal data for trainees, instructors, and administrators in alignment with Republic Act No. 10173.

Effective Date: November 12, 2026 Policy Version: 2026-11-v1

1. Personal Information Collected

  • Account & Identity Data: Full name, email address, mobile number, Seafarer Registration Number (SRN), portal role, and encrypted authentication credentials.
  • Enrollment & Attendance Records: Course applications, training batches, schedules, and attendance logs.
  • Assessment & Certification Records: Learning progress, exam/practical submissions, grading remarks, and verifiable certificate codes.
  • Payment Records: Transaction amounts, payment methods, reference numbers, PayMongo checkout identifiers, and official receipts (raw card numbers/CVVs are never stored on NEWSIM servers).

2. How Data Is Processed

Trainee data supports eligibility checks, course delivery, grading, and certificate issuance. Instructor data supports course instruction, evaluations, and leave scheduling. Administrator and Super Administrator data supports operations, financial verification (strictly permission-gated), and security auditing.

3. Supabase Storage, Security & Access Restrictions

NEWSIM persists records in Supabase (PostgreSQL, Auth, and Storage) using TLS encryption in transit, Role-Based Access Control (RBAC), Row-Level Security policies, HTTP-only session cookies, TOTP Multi-Factor Authentication, account lockouts, rate limiting, and Google reCAPTCHA.

4. Cookies & Consent Policy

Essential cookies maintain authenticated portal sessions and security boundaries. Optional Google Analytics 4 measurements remain blocked until explicit consent is given, and NEWSIM strips passwords, SRNs, emails, payment details, and assessment answers from analytics events. You can manage preferences anytime via Cookie Settings.

5. Your Rights Under RA 10173

You have the right to be informed, access, rectify inaccurate records, object or request erasure where lawful, seek data portability, and file grievances with the Philippine National Privacy Commission (NPC).

6. Data Retention & Account Deletion

Records are retained only as long as required for active maritime training delivery, certificate verification, financial auditability, and legal accountability. To request export, correction, or account deletion, contact NEWSIM using your registered email and SRN.
