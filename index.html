ENROLLMENT PROCESS

Experience a seamless, fully digital enrollment workflow from registration to dashboard activation.

01

Create Account

Register with your personal details and create secure login credentials through our digital portal.

02

Choose Program

Browse available maritime training programs and select the course that matches your career goals.

03

Generate Payment QR

Receive a unique QR code linked to your enrollment for secure, trackable payment processing.
