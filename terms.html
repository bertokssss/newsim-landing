Enrollments depend on seat capacity, prerequisites, valid SRN verification, and administrative confirmation.

Course fees must be settled via approved NEWSIM payment methods (including PayMongo checkout or verified branch settlement). Refunds or schedule transfers are evaluated under NEWSIM administrative review.

Trainees must attend scheduled simulator and classroom sessions and satisfy minimum attendance hours to qualify for completion.
